# SAA-C03 Practice Exam Resources

> [!info] Exam snapshot
>
> - **Exam code:** SAA-C03 (current version, replaced SAA-C02 in August 2022)
> - **Format:** 65 questions (50 scored + 15 unscored), 130 minutes
> - **Passing score:** 720 / 1000 (scaled)
> - **Question types:** multiple choice (1 of 4) and multiple response (2 of 5, 3 of 6). No partial credit on multiple-response questions.

---

## 1. Official Resources (AWS Skill Builder)

AWS gives away its Official Practice Question Sets and Exam Prep courses. The full Official Practice Exam needs a Skill Builder subscription.

| Resource                                     | Cost         | Notes                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Official Practice Question Set (SAA-C03)** | Free         | 20 questions written by AWS, aligned to SAA-C03. Closest free match to real exam style. Also available in other languages, including Traditional Chinese. |
| **Exam Prep Standard Course (SAA-C03)**      | Free         | Domain-by-domain walkthrough of question style and approach. Pair with the Question Set.                                                                  |
| **Official Practice Exam (SAA-C03)**         | Subscription | Full-length exam with the same question style and rigor as the real exam. Scores you pass/fail the way the real exam does.                                |
| **Exam Prep Enhanced Course**                | Subscription | Adds bonus questions, flashcards, builder labs, and the Official Practice Exam.                                                                           |

> [!tip] Subscription strategy
> Third-party sources list Skill Builder pricing at about **$29/month or $449/year**. Check the official site for current pricing. A cost-efficient approach is to subscribe for a single month in the final week before the exam, take the Official Practice Exam, then cancel.

---

## 4. Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Finish most of the core course** (e.g., Adrian Cantrill's SAA-C03 course).
2. **Take the free Official Practice Question Set (20 Qs)** to calibrate on real exam style.
3. **Drill with Tutorials Dojo**:
   - Use section/topic mode to target weak domains first.
   - Switch to timed mode for the final few full exams.
   - Benchmark: consistently scoring **80%+** in timed mode is a strong readiness signal.
4. **Final week:** subscribe to Skill Builder and take the **Official Practice Exam** as the last check.

---

## 5. Avoid Exam Dumps

> [!warning]
> Stay away from "real exam dump" sites. They are low quality, often have **wrong answers**, and violate the AWS Certification Agreement. Getting caught can lead to **certification revocation**.
